# Writing tips

These tips draw on the [Google developer documentation style guide](https://developers.google.com/style/), the [GOV.UK writing guidance](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/writing-guidelines/right-tone/), and the [Microsoft Writing Style Guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/style-guide/).

1. **Lead with the reader’s task.** State what the page helps someone do before background details. [Microsoft: get to the point](https://learn.microsoft.com/style-guide/top-10-tips-style-voice)
2. **Use familiar, concrete words.** Explain game or technical terms when readers might not know them. [Google: voice and tone](https://developers.google.com/style/tone)
3. **Give each sentence one main idea.** Split long explanations into shorter sentences or lists. [Microsoft: writing tips](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/global-communications/writing-tips)
4. **Prefer active voice.** Name who performs an action so readers can follow the steps. [Google: style guide highlights](https://developers.google.com/style/highlights)
5. **Be specific.** Include observed facts, examples, and meaningful limits instead of broad claims. [GOV.UK: content principles](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/govuk-content-principles-conventions-and-research-background/govuk-content-principles-conventions-and-research-background)
6. **Label sections clearly.** Use concise, descriptive headings in sentence case. [Google: headings and titles](https://developers.google.com/style/headings)
7. **Keep the tone conversational and respectful.** Avoid slang, clichés, and forced jokes. [Google: voice and tone](https://developers.google.com/style/tone)
8. **Address readers directly when useful.** Use “you” to make instructions easier to follow. [Google: style guide highlights](https://developers.google.com/style/highlights)
9. **Cut filler and repetition.** Remove warm-up phrases, repeated points, and sentences that do not help the reader. [Google: voice and tone](https://developers.google.com/style/tone)
10. **Revise for natural rhythm.** Read the draft aloud, fix awkward sentences, and vary repeated sentence openings. [Google: voice and tone](https://developers.google.com/style/tone)

The README applies these practices by leading with the ledger’s purpose, organizing around publishing and interpreting scans, and stating measurement limits directly. Its game-aware language describes the product without claiming player experience or live market knowledge.
