A note from the auctioneer

Good data. Better decisions.

One or two scans a day

Scan, use /reload or log out to flush SavedVariables, then run Auction Ledger Publisher on your PC. The local app calculates all trend periods and pushes sanitized results to this site. Price comparisons use the latest observation and the nearest scan at or before the selected lookback, falling back to the earliest available scan when history is shorter. The exact reference date appears below the chart. Each source, realm and faction stays separate. Auctionator cache prices may predate their capture time; daily lows are day buckets, not exact scan times.

What the numbers mean

Unit buyout is the cheapest observed buyout divided by stack size. The market index is an equal-weight fixed basket of items present at both endpoints, normalized to 100 at the reference. Sparse intermediate scans are omitted and counted in the caption; lines connect measured observations. Listed supply measures available units in buyout listings.

A listing is not a sale

A scan cannot distinguish a sold auction from one that expired or was cancelled. This ledger therefore ranks listed volume and potential resale spreads, not invented sales. Realized profit requires purchase and sale records.

Your files stay yours

The publisher reads your addon files without changing them. Only sanitized market observations and calculated trends are pushed: no seller names, character details, account paths, addon settings or deploy keys. Your PC can be off after publishing; GitHub Pages serves the static dashboard. Watchlists and the optional trade calculator work in your browser.
