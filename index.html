Auctioneer, Auctionator, CSV or an exported ledger. Parsing happens on your device; original files are never uploaded.

Auctioneer full scans

Finish a full Auction House scan, then log out or use /reload. Import WTF/Account/…/SavedVariables/Auc-ScanData.lua. Listing stacks become unit prices and supply counts; seller names are discarded.

Auctionator price history & CSV

Import Auctionator.lua for database v8 / realm v2 (including CBOR), or a CSV using our template. The capture time above dates the cached-price snapshot; individual prices may be older. Daily lows are a separate market source, dated in your browser’s local time zone. Daily maximum availability is not current supply, so quantities remain unknown. Names outside the built-in catalog appear as item IDs.

Keep history & publish it

Import once or twice daily; repeat imports are deduplicated. Browser storage preserves your history. In Scan library, export scans.json and upload it to the repo’s data/ directory to share the updated dashboard across devices.

A note from the auctioneer

Good data. Better decisions.

One or two scans a day

Scan, log out to flush SavedVariables, and import. Price comparisons use the latest observation and the nearest scan at or before the selected lookback, falling back to the earliest available scan when history is shorter. The exact reference date appears below the chart. Each source, realm and faction stays separate. Auctionator cache prices may predate their capture time; daily lows are day buckets, not exact scan times.

What the numbers mean

Unit buyout is the cheapest observed buyout divided by stack size. The market index is an equal-weight fixed basket of items present at both endpoints, normalized to 100 at the reference. Sparse intermediate scans are omitted and counted in the caption; lines connect measured observations. Listed supply measures available units in buyout listings.

A listing is not a sale

A scan cannot distinguish a sold auction from one that expired or was cancelled. This ledger therefore ranks listed volume and potential resale spreads, not invented sales. Realized profit requires purchase and sale records.

Your files stay yours

Imports are local to this browser. Only sanitized, aggregated price history is included in exported JSON: no seller names, character details or addon settings. Export regularly; clearing browser data also clears local imports. GitHub Pages serves a public static site.
