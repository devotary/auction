Help

Using the ledger

Update your scans

Open the Auction House and run /ald scan. Wait for the scanner to say Saved, then use /reload and run Auction Ledger Publisher on your PC. Refresh this page after publishing finishes.

Compare prices

Choose a market and period. Changes compare the latest observation with the nearest scan at or before that lookback, or the earliest scan when history is shorter. The reference date appears below the chart. The market index uses a fixed basket present at both endpoints; sparse observations are omitted.

Read supply and spreads

Listed supply counts available units, not completed sales. Missing counts remain unknown. Potential spreads compare the current price with the observed median after a 5% fee; they do not guarantee a sale or profit.

Compare auction houses

Click Scan on the Auction House window, or open /ald, to see the detected house. Use Scan current when recognized; for an unknown auctioneer, a saved manual scan teaches its house. Use /ald scan faction at the faction AH and /ald scan neutral at the Neutral AH, saving and reloading after each. Run Auction Ledger Flips or the publisher. In Flips, choose a direction. The latest Faction and Neutral scans are compared across accounts. Both scan times are shown; the default age limit is 24 hours. Margins include the destination sale fee shown on each row and exclude lost deposits, transfer costs and further undercutting. Match the house label to the AH you actually opened.

In-game item prices

ALD supplies saved prices to Auctionator’s existing Auction line, using its coin icons and Vendor line. Normal hover shows one item; Shift-hover shows the whole stack. Only ALD needs to scan. Prices update on save; desktop publishing updates this website.

Keep a watchlist

Use the checkbox beside an item to save it to your watchlist. Watchlists stay in this browser.
